THE GRAND HAVEN DIFFERENCE

What sets Grand Haven apart

Intentionally Intimate

A six-resident setting allows for a more personal environment where residents can be known as individuals and supported according to their established needs and routines.

Experienced Care Team

Our caregiving team brings extensive experience in residential care, including caregivers with more than 30 years and 10 years of experience.

Clinically Informed Leadership. Personally Led.

Grand Haven pairs the intimacy of a private residence with the insight of advanced clinical experience and the attentiveness of hands-on healthcare leadership.

Our owner and administrator brings an advanced healthcare background spanning emergency care, intensive care, and psychiatry, offering a well-rounded perspective on changing needs, complex care considerations, resident advocacy, and communication with families and healthcare professionals.

As an owner-operated residence with leadership onsite, we remain closely involved in each resident’s transition, ongoing needs, and overall experience. Care is thoughtfully coordinated within the scope of residential care, with an emphasis on dignity, continuity, and individualized attention.

A private residence, thoughtfully led by someone who understands the complexities of care

Elevated Everyday Living

From thoughtfully prepared meals and comfortable surroundings to engaging activities and meaningful social experiences, attention is given to the details that make daily life feel welcoming and personal.

A Residential Setting

A beautiful, carefully maintained home designed to offer the warmth and familiarity of residential living rather than the atmosphere of a large institutional setting.
