RESIDENT PROFILE

Grand Haven may be considered for adults who:

  • Require assistance with activities of daily living
  • Would benefit from a smaller residential setting
  • Have appropriate dementia-related care needs
  • Are seeking private-pay residential care
  • May be appropriate for hospice services, subject to individual assessment and the home’s ability to safely accommodate their needs

Every prospective resident is individually assessed prior to admission. Admission is subject to availability, applicable licensing requirements, and Grand Haven’s ability to safely and appropriately meet the resident’s needs.
